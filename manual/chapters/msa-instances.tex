\chapter{Multiple sequence alignment and the sets of instances}%
\label{ch:msa-instances}

\begin{center}
\fbox{\parbox{0.9\linewidth}{\textsc{Stub.} This chapter is written
together with the code it describes, which is not in the library yet.}}
\end{center}

We are concerned in this chapter with the Multiple Sequence Alignment (MSA)
problem as a shortest path on a lattice, with its pairwise heuristic, which is
the standard test of a parallel search with a large branching factor and a
costly heuristic, and with the sets of instances on which the searches of this
manual are compared (the 15-puzzle and the 24-puzzle, the grids, the traveling
salesman and the alignments), with their generators and the format of their
files.
