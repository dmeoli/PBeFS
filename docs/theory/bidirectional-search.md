# Bidirectional search

The front-to-end bidirectional searches of the library are treated in
[chapter 7](https://dmeoli.github.io/PBeFS/manual/chapters/07-bidirectional.html) of the [manual](https://dmeoli.github.io/PBeFS/manual/index.html): the two fronts, their meeting and the
lower bounds that stop a search in [sections 7.2 and 7.3](https://dmeoli.github.io/PBeFS/manual/chapters/07-bidirectional.html#sec-7-2); the
bidirectional Dijkstra, MM, MMe, fMM and BAE* in [section 7.4](https://dmeoli.github.io/PBeFS/manual/chapters/07-bidirectional.html#sec-7-4); NBS and the
must-expand graph in [section 7.5](https://dmeoli.github.io/PBeFS/manual/chapters/07-bidirectional.html#sec-7-5); DVCBS in [section 7.6](https://dmeoli.github.io/PBeFS/manual/chapters/07-bidirectional.html#sec-7-6); GBFHS and the bidirectional
breadth-first search in [section 7.7](https://dmeoli.github.io/PBeFS/manual/chapters/07-bidirectional.html#sec-7-7); the two directions on two workers in
[section 7.8](https://dmeoli.github.io/PBeFS/manual/chapters/07-bidirectional.html#sec-7-8); and the parallel searches on external memory (PEM-A*, PEM-rA*, PEMM,
PEM-BAE*) and A* with immediate duplicate detection on the disk in
[sections 7.9 and 7.10](https://dmeoli.github.io/PBeFS/manual/chapters/07-bidirectional.html#sec-7-9).
