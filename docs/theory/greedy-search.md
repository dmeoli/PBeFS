# Greedy best-first search and its parallelization

Greedy best-first search, its OPEN lists, enforced hill-climbing, the iterated
searches of LAMA and the width-based searches are treated in [sections 2.12 to
2.15](https://dmeoli.github.io/PBeFS/manual/chapters/02-best-first.html#sec-2-12)
of the [manual](https://dmeoli.github.io/PBeFS/manual/index.html). The parallel
greedy searches (KPGBFS, PUHF and its variants, SPUHF, OBAT, the separate
generation and evaluation, HDGBFS) are in [section
12.8](https://dmeoli.github.io/PBeFS/manual/chapters/12-more-parallel-schemes.html#sec-12-8),
together with the bench transition system and the bounds that each one keeps on
the expansions.
