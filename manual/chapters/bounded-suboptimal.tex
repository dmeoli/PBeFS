\chapter{Bounded-suboptimal and anytime search}\label{ch:bounded-suboptimal}

\begin{center}
\fbox{\parbox{0.9\linewidth}{\textsc{Stub.} This chapter is written
together with the code it describes, which is not in the library yet.}}
\end{center}

We are concerned in this chapter with the searches that trade the optimality of
the solution for a smaller number of expansions, while still bounding its cost:
weighted A$^*$, whose solution costs at most $w$ times the optimum when the
heuristic is admissible (and $w \ge 1$), the parallel searches that keep this
bound (e.g., weighted PBNF and weighted HDA$^*$), and the anytime searches
(e.g., anytime repairing A$^*$), which find a first solution quickly and then
improve it, together with a lower bound on the optimum, until they prove it
optimal or are stopped.
