# Pattern databases

The pattern databases of the sliding-tile puzzles are treated in [chapter
5](https://dmeoli.github.io/PBeFS/manual/chapters/05-heuristics.html) of the
[manual](https://dmeoli.github.io/PBeFS/manual/index.html), together with the other heuristics of the library. Its subjects are the abstraction of a pattern under the two
rules of the blank of the library, the admissibility and the consistency of the
sum over a partition and of its maximum with the reflection about the main
diagonal, the encodings of the entries from one byte down to one bit, and the
construction of the tables. Also, the pseudocode of the construction and of the lookup is on the page of
[PatternDatabase.h](../../include/PBeFS/astar/PatternDatabase.h).
