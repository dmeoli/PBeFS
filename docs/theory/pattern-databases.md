# Pattern databases

The pattern databases of the sliding-tile puzzles (the abstraction of a
pattern under the two rules of the blank of the library, the admissibility and
the consistency of the sum over a partition and of its maximum with the
reflection about the main diagonal, the encodings of the entries from one byte
down to one bit, and the construction of the tables) are treated in
[chapter 5](https://dmeoli.github.io/PBeFS/manual/chapters/05-heuristics.html) of the [manual](https://dmeoli.github.io/PBeFS/manual/index.html), together
with the other heuristics of the library. The pseudocode of the construction
and of the lookup is also on the page of
[PatternDatabase.h](../../include/PBeFS/astar/PatternDatabase.h).
