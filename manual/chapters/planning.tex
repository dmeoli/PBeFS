\chapter{Classical planning}\label{ch:planning}

\begin{center}
\fbox{\parbox{0.9\linewidth}{\textsc{Stub.} This chapter is written
together with the code it describes, which is not in the library yet.}}
\end{center}

We are concerned in this chapter with classical planning as a domain of the
library: a task in STRIPS form, whose states are sets of facts and whose
operators have preconditions, additions and deletions, the domain-independent
heuristics computed on it, and the distributions of HDA$^*$ that use the
structure of the task (e.g., the abstraction of a subset of its variables)
instead of that of a particular puzzle.
